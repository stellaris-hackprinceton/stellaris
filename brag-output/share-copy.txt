Ask Stellaris a research question and it does the science: searches arXiv and pulls the full papers, designs the experiment, writes and runs the analysis in a sandboxed container, records experiments from your webcam, and writes the report. On 25,000 real records it found r = 0.503 and fit W = −37.46 + 55.06 × H.

Stellaris: from question to discovery. Built at HackPrinceton Fall '26 with LangChain Deep Agents on Amazon Bedrock.
